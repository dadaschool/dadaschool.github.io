Talking about future jobs and reasons

Today's Goals

By the end of this class…

  1. I can talk about my dream job.
  2. I can give a reason with because.
  3. I can write three sentences about my dream job.
목표를 함께 소리 내어 읽습니다. 수업 끝에 세 문장 쓰기로 확인한다고 미리 알려 줍니다. (1분)

Key Expressions

Ask and answer.

A

What do you want to be in the future?

B

I want to be a vet.

A

Why do you want to be a vet?

B

Because I love animals.

교사가 A, 학생 전체가 B를 맡아 읽고 역할을 바꿉니다. 말로 짧게 답할 때는 «Because ~.» 로 시작해도 되지만, 글로 쓸 때는 «I want to be a vet because I love animals.» 처럼 한 문장으로 잇는다고 짚습니다. (5분)
Grammar Point

a or an?

a + consonant sound

a doctor · a vet
a chef · a game designer

an + vowel sound

an engineer · an artist
an astronaut

Listen to the sound, not the letter: a uniform, an hour

모음 «소리» 앞에서는 an 을 씁니다. 철자가 아니라 소리가 기준이라는 점을 uniform(유-), hour(아워-)로 보여 줍니다. 예문: I want to be an engineer because I like making things. (4분)
Reading

Jiho's Dream Job

Hi, I'm Jiho. I want to be a game designer. I like playing games, and I love drawing characters. I want to make games that help students learn. To make my dream come true, I draw every day and I'm learning to code.

1. What does Jiho want to be?

2. Why does Jiho want to do this job?

3. What does Jiho do every day?

1분 동안 조용히 읽고 질문에 답합니다. 정답: 1. a game designer 2. Jiho likes playing games and loves drawing characters. Jiho wants to make games that help students learn. 3. Jiho draws every day. (6분)
Pair Talk

Talk with your partner.

  • ① Ask: What do you want to be in the future?
  • ② Ask Why? and listen for because.
  • ③ Switch roles.

Helpful sentences

  • I want to be a(n) ____.
  • I like ____. / I'm good at ____.
  • I'm not sure yet, but I'm interested in ____.
짝과 2분씩 묻고 답합니다. 돌아다니며 a/an 과 because 를 쓰는지 듣습니다. 꿈이 아직 없는 학생은 «I'm not sure yet, but I'm interested in ~.» 으로 말해도 된다고 알려 줍니다. (8분)
Writing

Write about your dream job.

  1. In the future, I want to be a(n) .
  2. I want to do this job because .
  3. To make my dream come true, I will .

Example: In the future, I want to be a vet. I want to do this job because I love animals. To make my dream come true, I will read books about animals.

학습지나 공책에 세 문장을 씁니다. 다 쓴 학생은 짝과 바꿔 a/an, because, 마침표를 서로 확인합니다. (10분)
