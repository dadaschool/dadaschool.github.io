[Fictional text for teacher training] The School Garden

Our school has a small garden behind the gym. Every spring, students in the science club plant tomatoes, lettuce, and sunflowers. They water the plants before class and write notes about how tall each plant grows.

Last year, the club had a problem. Some plants did not get enough sunlight because the gym wall made a long shadow in the afternoon. The students measured the shadow every week and moved the sunflowers to a sunnier place.

In summer, the club shared the lettuce with the school cafeteria. The cook made a fresh salad, and many students tried it for the first time. One student said, "I never liked lettuce, but this one tastes different because we grew it."

The garden teaches students that small changes, like moving a plant a few meters, can make a big difference.
